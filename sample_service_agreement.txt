SERVICE AGREEMENT


1. Scope of Services
The service provider shall deliver software development and maintenance services as per the requirements communicated by the client.


2. Payment Terms
The client shall pay the service provider a fixed fee of INR 5,00,000 within 120 days from the date of invoice.


3. Term and Termination
This agreement shall be valid for a period of 24 months. The client may terminate this agreement at any time without cause by providing 7 days’ notice. The service provider may terminate only in case of material breach.


4. Intellectual Property
All intellectual property created during the course of this agreement shall be the sole and exclusive property of the client.


5. Confidentiality
Both parties shall maintain strict confidentiality of all business information shared during the term of this agreement.


6. Indemnity
The service provider agrees to indemnify the client against any claims, losses, damages, or expenses arising out of the services provided.


7. Governing Law and Jurisdiction
This agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of courts in Bengaluru.
